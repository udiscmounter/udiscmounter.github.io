Operation : Mount & Eject files

1.Mount

Right-click on your disc file (ISO, IMG, and others), then you will see 2 uDiscMounter options. Choose "Mount with uDiscMounter" to mount directly without specifying a virtual drive, or "Mount to uDiscMounter" to mount by selecting the virtual drive location.

Alternatively, it can be done through the program window by first selecting the file in the list table, then clicking the "Mount" button.

2. Eject

To eject a file from a virtual drive, you can do it directly through File Explorer/manager by right-clicking on the mounted drive, then selecting "Eject".

Or through the program, by selecting the drive you want to eject and then clicking the "Eject" button.
